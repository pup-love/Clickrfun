A COMMUNITY MADE WITH LOVE

About Clickr ♡

Clickr is a puppy-themed social community concept built around making friends, finding your pack, and discovering little secrets.

We're building it one pawprint at a time. Some features are still demos while we work toward real member accounts and chat.

Our pack rules 🐾

♡ Respect boundaries and consent.
♡ Be kind to other members.
♡ Don't share private information.
♡ Block and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.

Clickr ♡ made with love, friendship, and pawprints.
