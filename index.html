Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to find your paws around Clickrfun.

What is Clickrfun?

Clickrfun is a little social corner of the internet with profiles, chat rooms, private messages, virtual pets, badges, treats, a shop, and games.

How do I make an account?

Open the Login section and choose the sign-up option. Follow the prompts to create your account. If confirmation is requested, check your email for the sign-up link.

How do I chat with other members?

Sign in, then choose a public room from the navigation. You can also open Private DMs to message another member when that feature is available to your account.

What are treats?

Treats are virtual rewards used around Clickrfun. Check My Treats and the Shop to see the options currently available to you.

How do badges and stickers work?

Explore the site and try the available activities to unlock collectibles. Your collection shows the badges or stickers you've earned.

How do virtual pets work?

Visit My Virtual Puppy or My Virtual Kitty to meet and care for your virtual companion. Check their needs and use the available pet actions.

Can I use Clickrfun on my phone?

Clickrfun is designed to fit different screen sizes. If something looks odd on your device, try refreshing the page or using an up-to-date browser.

How can I keep my account safe?

Never share your password or private account details with other members. Use a unique password and only enter your login details on the official Clickrfun website.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Refresh the page and try again. If the issue continues, note what you clicked and what happened, then share that information with the site owner so it can be investigated.

Still stuck? Try refreshing Clickrfun, then ask for help with the exact thing that isn't working.
